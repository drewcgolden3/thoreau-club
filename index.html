About the club

A second home with a pool in it.

The Thoreau Club exists to build a real sense of community across every program we run, an encouraging place for members and staff alike. Families come here to play tennis, train, swim, unwind at the spa and spend actual time together.

You will find us on Forest Ridge Road in Concord, with tennis courts, a full fitness floor, pools, a spa and one of the busiest summer camps in the area.
